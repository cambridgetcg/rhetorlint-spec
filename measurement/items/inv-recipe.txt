Simple Lentil Soup (serves 4)

Ingredients: 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks, 2 garlic cloves, 200 g red lentils, 1 litre vegetable stock, 1 tin chopped tomatoes, 1 teaspoon ground cumin, salt and pepper.

1. Chop the onion, carrots and celery. Peel and crush the garlic.
2. Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Add the vegetables and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft.
3. Stir in the garlic and cumin and cook for 1 minute.
4. Rinse the lentils, then add them to the pan with the stock and tomatoes. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 20 to 25 minutes, until the lentils are tender.
5. Season with salt and pepper. Blend half the soup if you like it thicker, or leave it chunky.

The soup keeps for 3 days in the fridge and can be frozen for up to 3 months. If it thickens too much when reheated, add a splash of water.
