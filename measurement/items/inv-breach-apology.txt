Subject: An important update about your account

Dear Valued Customer,

At Brightwell, the trust you place in us is the foundation of everything we do. We are writing to let you know about a recent security incident that may have involved some of your information.

On 3 March, we became aware of suspicious activity on a third-party system operated by one of our vendors. Upon discovery, we immediately launched an investigation with the support of leading cybersecurity experts and notified law enforcement. Based on our investigation to date, certain customer information may have been accessed, including names, email addresses, phone numbers and, for a limited number of customers, partial payment card details. There is currently no evidence that this information has been misused.

We take the protection of your personal information extremely seriously and sincerely apologize for any inconvenience or concern this may cause. Out of an abundance of caution, we are offering affected customers 12 months of complimentary credit monitoring. Additional safeguards have been put in place to help prevent a similar incident from happening again.

Protecting your data has always been, and will always be, our top priority. We remain committed to earning your trust every day.

If you have any questions, our dedicated support team is here to help.

Sincerely,
The Brightwell Customer Care Team
