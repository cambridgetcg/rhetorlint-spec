Counterfactual transform

Deterministic, no model: lexical intensifiers are removed and passives with omitted agents are flagged with [who?]. Modality and attribution stay intact. This makes a comparison variant; it is not guaranteed to preserve meaning or truth.

How to read this tool within its limits

  • DOESMark configured patterns in the words — every mark points at a real, visible phrase you can see and check.
  • DOES NOTRead the person. It infers no tone, no intent, no deception. A marker is not a finding.
  • DOES NOTJudge whether a claim is factually true. That is your job, and the world's — not a pattern-matcher's.
  • DOES NOTProve an effect on a recipient. Effect descriptions are hypotheses until tested for a declared context and substrate.
  • DOESReport an uncalibrated rule weight in the legacy confidence field — never a probability of intent, effect, deception, or truth.

Density is only a count from the configured pack. A high or low value does not establish intent, effect, deception, harm, or truth. RhetorLint gives you observations and questions, not a verdict.
